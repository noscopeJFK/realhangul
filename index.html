Learn Korean romanization, one Jamo at a time

Hangul is built from 40 Jamo — 19 consonants and 21 vowels. Each Jamo has a fixed romanization sound and a fixed key on the standard 2-set (두벌식) keyboard — the layout used by Microsoft, Google, and most other Korean IMEs. In practice you can drill either the 2-set key(s) or the romanization — switch with the toggle above the game.

Click cards to select the Jamo you want to drill, then hit “Practice selection”.
